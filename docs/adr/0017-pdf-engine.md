# ADR 0017 — PDF engine (after the M0.5 spike: react-pdf vs Gotenberg)

- **Status:** Proposed — pending the M0.5 PDF spike (rationale from roadmap §3.1)

## Context
- Tickets, invoices, badges and reports are PDFs.
- They must render Arabic (RTL shaping), Hindi (Devanagari) and CJK text correctly.
- Accessibility requires tagged PDFs (roadmap §10).
- Legacy ticket and invoice PDF URLs must keep resolving (parity matrix).

## Decision (proposed)
- Run the **M0.5 PDF spike**: Arabic, Hindi and Japanese tickets, plus tagged-PDF output, with two candidates:
  - `@react-pdf/renderer`
  - Gotenberg (Chromium)
- **Expected result:** Gotenberg for tickets and reports.
- If Gotenberg wins, it runs on Fly next to the worker (ADR 0004); PDFs are generated by the worker.
- The engine sits behind `packages/pdf` so it can be swapped.

## Alternatives
- **`@react-pdf/renderer`:** in-process and light, but shaping and tagging for complex scripts is the open question.
- **Gotenberg/Chromium:** strong shaping and HTML/CSS reuse; costs an extra service.

## Consequences
- The spike result updates this ADR to Accepted (or records the other choice).
- Ticket PDFs carry the Ed25519 QR and short code (ADR 0011).
- Theme tokens are shared with PDFs (roadmap §4.4).

## Revisit when
- The M0.5 spike completes.
- PDF volume or latency exceeds worker capacity.
