## Scope (each fully tested)
- A **Money** nav group (from U2) with:
  - **Overview**: gross, fees, refunds, net, payouts; chart by day/week/month in the org time zone; period picker; comparison to the previous period.
  - **Payouts**: upcoming and past payouts as a timeline, held reserve with release date, receivables, each payout drilling down to its orders, refunds and fees.
  - **Sales by event**: table with gross/net/tickets, links to each event's finance analysis.
  - **Fees**: the org's fee plan (from billing, staff-set; UX-4) and fees taken per order and per payout.
  - CSV export for each.
- Reuses `orgReportQuery`, `orgFinanceQuery`, `settlementsQuery`, `receivablesQuery`; the old prose sections become a help panel.

## Acceptance
- Totals match the ledger to the cent on the fixture org (integration test).
- A payout drills down to exactly its orders.
- Only roles with finance/reports permission see the group; exports respect the same.
- E2E: overview with period change, a payout drill-down, sales by event, a CSV export; keyboard only, axe in both themes, RTL. Attach before/after screenshots (light and dark, 1280 and 390 px) of the main screens to your report (commit them under `docs/ux/screenshots/u5/`).
