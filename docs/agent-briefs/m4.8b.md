## Scope (each fully tested)
- **Charity profile** per org: legal name, EIN, 501(c)(3) exempt status, optional fiscal sponsor (whose details then appear on receipts).
- **Verification by staff** in the admin app against the IRS exempt-organization list:
  - an `ExemptOrgLookup` port with a fake in CI and a recorded fixture
  - the real list download is an owner-run job, no live calls in tests
  - every staff action audited
- **Fair-market value** (FMV) per ticket type. On ticket pages over $75 for verified charities, the **quid-pro-quo notice** ("of your $500 payment, $350 is tax-deductible").
- **Receipts** by email and PDF in every locale, per payment:
  - the deductible amount = amount − FMV
  - "No goods or services were provided" for pure gifts
  - **plain "not tax-deductible" receipts** for unverified orgs
  - a **year-end statement** per donor (a scheduled job, org timezone)
- Receipt text comes from a template marked `legal-copy`, in its own file, for counsel to review.
- **Privacy:** amounts and donor names follow P4-13. Receipts go only to the donor.

## Acceptance
- A $500 ticket with a $150 FMV gives a receipt with $350 deductible.
- A $100 gift with nothing in return says "No goods or services were provided".
- An unverified org issues only "not tax-deductible" receipts.
- **Golden PDFs in English and Arabic.**
- A year-end statement totals exactly.
- Isolation; impersonation and freeze coverage.
- E2E on all three projects:
  - create the profile; staff verify it (admin e2e)
  - set FMV; see the notice on the ticket page
  - give and receive the receipt in the dev mailbox
  - keyboard only, axe, RTL
