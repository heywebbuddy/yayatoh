## Scope (each fully tested)
- **Enrollments** live in `registration`, by registrant and session. **Availability** comes from the admission item: which sessions an item includes or allows.
- An **atomic claim** goes through program's capacity counter (single statement or row lock; the CHECK `enrolled ≤ capacity` is the last line of defence).
- **Session groups:** "pick one" is enforced.
- **Conflict checks:** overlapping sessions are refused with a clear message.
- **Session waitlist** with automatic promotion:
  - FIFO, offer and accept window, or auto-enrol per the event setting
  - a close time (P5-9): promotion stops 24 h before the start
  - a re-check of availability and conflicts on promotion
- **Attendee "My schedule" page** (mobile-first):
  - included sessions
  - enrolled optional sessions
  - the waitlist position
  - drop and join
- The organizer sees per-session enrolment, waitlist length, and a "promote now" action.

## Acceptance
- **No oversell under concurrency:** 200 parallel enrollments for 50 places give exactly 50 (integration test on real Postgres).
- **Waitlist promotion never loops** (property test).
- Promotion stops 24 h before the start.
- A session group allows exactly one pick.
- Isolation; impersonation and freeze coverage.
- E2E on all three projects:
  - enrol, conflict refused, group pick
  - join the waitlist, get promoted when someone drops
  - keyboard only, axe, RTL
  - viewer denied the organizer actions
