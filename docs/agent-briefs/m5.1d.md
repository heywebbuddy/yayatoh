## Scope (each fully tested)
- **Pay later** is an organizer opt-in **per registration type** (P5-5).
- **Order states** `awaiting_invoice → paid` or `void`, plus partial payments and the balance.
- **Invoices:** PDF numbered per org (gapless, never reused), optional or required PO number per type.
- **Terms default (P5-5):** Net 30 from the invoice date, but due no later than 7 days before the event starts.
- **Pay link:** a checkout on the org's normal funds flow, with an Idempotency-Key on every pay link and payment. Fake provider in tests; never call Stripe.
- **Offline payments** (check, wire) recorded by staff with a reference, audited. Organizer-collected, so the fee becomes a receivable, as today.
- **Reminders** on M3.7a journeys (before due, on due, overdue), stopping on payment.
- **Balance-due flag** shown on the badge and check-in screens, with an audited override.
- **An unpaid registration is never auto-cancelled.** The organizer decides.

## Acceptance
- A $1,200 invoice paid in two parts reconciles to the cent (ledger memo entries and fee receivable).
- An unpaid registration is never auto-cancelled.
- An idempotency key on every pay link: replaying it never double-charges.
- Invoice numbers are gapless under concurrency.
- Isolation; impersonation and freeze coverage.
- E2E on all three projects:
  - register with pay later and PO
  - receive the invoice PDF (dev mailbox)
  - pay half by link and record the rest offline
  - balance flag at check-in, with override
  - keyboard only, axe, RTL
  - viewer can't record payments
