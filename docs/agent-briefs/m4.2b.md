Also read `docs/plans/phase-4.md` (Wave B row M4.2b; the gala profile; P4-8), `docs/decisions.md`, `docs/specs/M4.2/spec.md`, `packages/modules/guests`, ticketing (ticket types, orders, the existing claim/transfer flows: reuse them, never copy them), seating (tables, the seat finder) and the gala profile's nav/vocabulary from M4.2a.

## Scope (each fully tested)
- **"Tables & Sponsors" and "Tickets" tabs** in the gala event workspace. They replace placeholders if any exist; nav entries go through the profile registry.
- **A table ticket type** (for example "Table of 10", with a size and price). Buying one creates that many **guest slots** linked to the order. The buyer names the guests through a **claim link**: signed, one per table, and the buyer can resend it. Each named slot becomes a guest in a party (the buyer's company or the sponsor) linked to its ticket.
- **Hosted tables:** a table can carry a **sponsor name** (and optional logo through media). The name shows on the table in the seating editor and in the public seat finder, through an allowlist serializer.
- **Guests are linked to tickets** both ways: a guest shows its ticket, a ticket shows its guest. Check-in still works per ticket.
- **Host view:**
  - tables list: sponsor, size, seats named, missing names
  - "send naming reminder"
  - manual naming by the host
- Out of scope: donations (M4.8a), paddle raise (M4.8c), receipts.

## Acceptance
- Buying a table of 10 gives exactly 10 guest slots. Concurrent claims never exceed the size.
- The sponsor name shows on the table in the editor and in the seat finder. The sponsor is never shown for unpublished tables.
- The claim link names only its table's slots. A refund removes the unnamed slots.
- Isolation and commands through `tenantCommand`.
- E2E on all three projects:
  - buy a table (fake provider), name guests via the link, see them on Guests and in seating
  - the sponsor name appears in the seat finder
  - keyboard only, axe, RTL
  - viewer denied
