## Scope (each fully tested)
- **One data-subject request** (export or erase) per person per org, staff- or self-initiated (verified email), with a due date (30 days) and status.
- **Export**: every module contributes through a `DataSubjectContributor` port (registered per module, public export). The result is one signed ZIP (JSON per module plus files) with a manifest.
- **Erase**:
  - each module erases or anonymises its rows through its contributor
  - projections are rebuilt or redacted
  - media files are deleted
  - connector hooks are called (no-op until M6.4)
  - **legal holds** keep orders, payments and tax receipts with the person's name replaced, listed in the receipt
- **Signed receipt** PDF listing what was erased and what was kept and why.
- The **coverage test** fails if any table with a private or PII column has no contributor (use the private-columns registry).
