## Scope (each fully tested)
- **Templates and brand kits published downward** from agency to clients (copied into the client org; the client owns its copy).
- **Per-client campaign fan-out**: one agency campaign sent as separate campaigns in each client org, each with that client's consent and sender rules.

- **Team and day-of grants**: time-boxed, revocable grants for agency staff on event day.
