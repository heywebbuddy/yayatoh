## Scope (each fully tested)
- **Permission prompt** at the P7-8 moments only: after the first purchase and when a ticket is first opened, never at launch. A pre-prompt explains what will be sent; "Not now" asks again only after a later purchase. Denying never blocks anything.
- **Registration** with `expo-notifications`: register the APNs/FCM token through `POST /v1/me/devices` (app id, version, language), refresh it when it changes, and delete it at sign-out. Fakes in tests; no real APNs or FCM.
- **Opening the right screen:** a tapped push (`data.path`) or a universal link opens its screen from a cold start, a warm start and the foreground, through `routeForPush` and `routeForLink`: ticket, order, claim, event. Links that need sign-in resume after it.
- **Notification settings** (`account/notifications.tsx`): the system permission state with a link to the phone's settings, and the kinds the person can turn off (reminders, event changes); marketing pushes stay off unless the person opts in through the consent ledger (P7-8).
- **Foreground pushes:** shown as an in-app banner, without personal data.

## Acceptance
- Every push type and link opens its screen from a cold start (component tests over the router; Maestro flows for each in the device pass).
- Denying permission never blocks a purchase, a ticket or sign-in; the app asks again only at the next P7-8 moment.
- Sign-out deletes the device registration; no push text contains an email, phone number, amount or ticket code.
