## Scope (each fully tested)
- **A mobile-first public RSVP page** (per P4-2), reached in one of three ways:
  - a signed party magic link (revocable, with expiry)
  - the same link as a QR code (a printable QR per party for the invitation)
  - the paper fallback: strict name lookup plus a PIN printed on the invitation, rate-limited (`limitAction`) with the M1.14 challenge
- **Household RSVP:** one person answers for the whole party, sub-event by sub-event, only for the sub-events the party is invited to. They name the plus-one where the party has a placeholder.
- **States per party and sub-event:** `invited → sent → viewed → responded` (with attending/declined counts); `viewed` is recorded on the first open.
- **Deadline per event:** after it the page is read-only, with a "contact the host" message. The host can reopen a party.
- Every change writes `rsvp_history` with source `rsvp_page` (or `host` when the host edits it).
- The RSVP status feeds `event_participation` through the outbox (`guests.rsvp_responded.v1`). Guests are never added to marketing audiences or campaigns (P4-3).
- **Host side:** the Guests page shows each party's RSVP state, with filters and a "copy link / show QR / reset PIN / reopen" menu. No sending of invitations here: email and SMS invitations are M4.1f.
- Out of scope: RSVP questions and meal choice (M4.1e), invitations and the contact collector (M4.1f).

## Acceptance
- Name lookup never reveals the guest list. Enumeration test: wrong or partial names and wrong PINs give the same response and timing class. After the limit, the challenge appears.
- An RSVP to a sub-event the party is not invited to is refused in the command, not only in the UI.
- After the deadline, writes are refused.
- The magic link works for its party only. A revoked link fails.
- Isolation, impersonation and freeze-mode coverage for the new commands.
- E2E on all three projects:
  - link, QR (decode the QR in the test) and name+PIN paths
  - household with a plus-one
  - declined
  - deadline locked
  - host reopen
  - keyboard only, axe, Arabic RTL
  - viewer cannot reset PINs
