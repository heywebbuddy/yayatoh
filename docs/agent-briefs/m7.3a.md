Read `common.md` (including its "Don't stall" section) and `mobile-common.md` (its Base section and "Wave 3"). Then read `docs/plans/mobile.md` (row M7.3a and decisions P7-5, P7-8), ADR 0025, and the code:

**Base:** `origin/main` after Wave 2 (see `mobile-common.md`). You own the attendee app's tab shell.

## Scope (each fully tested)
- **Tab shell:** `(tabs)/_layout.tsx` with Tickets, Discover and Account. Signed-out people see the welcome flow; signed-in people land on Tickets. The Tickets and Discover tabs are other builders' screens: add a placeholder only if theirs aren't merged yet.
- **Welcome:** what the app does, then "Continue with email", Sign in with Apple (iOS), Google, and "Use a password".
- **Sign-in:** every M7.2a path through `mobile-core`'s helpers:
  - email code: request, enter the 6 digits (paste and autofill), resend with the server's cooldown, wrong and expired codes
  - Apple and Google through their providers (fakes in tests)
  - password
  - the two-factor step (TOTP or a backup code) and the email-proof step for a new provider link
  - rate limits shown with the time to wait
- **Account screen:** name, email (read-only, shows verified), language (in-app override), theme (system, light, dark), a link to notification settings (M7.3d's screen), sign out, and "sign out everywhere".
- **Account deletion** (App Review 5.1.1(v)): explain what is deleted and what the organizers keep, require step-up, delete, sign out and wipe local data. `/v1` has no deletion route yet: add `DELETE /v1/me` over the web's `deleteAccount` (`packages/modules/privacy/src/account.ts`), requiring a fresh step-up (M7.2a's `POST /v1/auth/step-up`), with integration tests, OpenAPI and the SDK regenerated. That makes this branch an **auth** change too: say so in your report, and `common.md`'s gate applies to the server part.
- **First launch offline:** a clear state with retry, never a blank screen.

## Acceptance
- Every sign-in path reaches Tickets through the fakes, including the two-factor and email-proof steps; wrong codes and rate limits show their own messages.
- `DELETE /v1/me` refuses without a fresh step-up and deletes with one, exactly as the web does (integration test); the app then holds no tokens or cache.
- Sign out everywhere revokes the refresh family; the app returns to welcome.
- Every control has a screen-reader label and role; Arabic renders right-to-left (component tests). Maestro flows: email-code sign-in, password with two-factor, delete account.
