## Scope (each fully tested)
- **The new `engagement` module** (tier per the module rules), with a MODULE.md.
- **Server-authoritative polls per session:** single choice, multi choice, rating, word cloud (stored as counts).
  - States: draft → open → closed, plus "show results".
- **Moderated Q&A per session:**
  - submit, upvote, approve or dismiss, mark answered
  - an anonymous option (the host still can't see the identity unless the policy allows it)
  - rate limits
- **Views:**
  - **participant** (mobile-first, from the session page or a QR)
  - **moderator** (queue, approve, pin)
  - **presenter** (the current question and poll)
  - **big screen** (a signed display link, reconnect snapshot, reduced motion and high contrast)
- **Realtime** goes over the M3.1b publisher, with org- and session-scoped channels.

## Acceptance
- One vote per person per poll under concurrency (integration test).
- Unapproved questions never reach public payloads. Extend the leak crawler or canary.
- The big screen recovers after a dropped connection with no missed state.
- A cross-org channel attach is denied.
- Isolation; impersonation and freeze coverage.
- E2E on all three projects:
  - create and open a poll; vote from two contexts; results update live
  - submit a question, moderate it, see it on the big screen
  - anonymous question
  - keyboard only, axe, RTL
  - viewer denied moderation
