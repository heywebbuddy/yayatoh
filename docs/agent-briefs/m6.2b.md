## Scope (each fully tested)
- **Multi-touch attribution**: first-touch, last-touch and linear models over touches (campaign sends/clicks, tracking links, UTMs, referrers) within a documented lookback window; results stored as warehouse rollups; integer minor units with a documented remainder rule for linear splits.
- A **curated explorer**: a fixed set of measures and dimensions (no free SQL), saved views per user, CSV export through an allowlist serializer.
- **Organizer-authored alert rules** on the M3.2b engine (threshold or change over a window; quiet hours in the recipient's time zone).
- **Scheduled PDF reports** by email (daily/weekly/monthly) in the **org time zone**; one send per period, deduplicated by (rule, period) key; the PDF is rendered in the recipient's locale.

## Acceptance
- A fixture campaign's attributed revenue matches **hand-computed numbers** for all three models, to the cent.
- A scheduled report **arrives once per period** even when the worker restarts or the job retries; DST changes don't skip or double a period.
- Isolation; finance gating on attribution money.
