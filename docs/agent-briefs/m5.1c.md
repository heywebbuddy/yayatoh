## Scope (each fully tested)
- **Apply-to-attend** per registration type, with states `pending → approved → confirmed` or `denied`.
  - **Auto-approve rules:** email domain, or a member list (CSV upload or audience).
  - Pending applicants are **never charged**. Payment happens on approval via a pay link (idempotent), or free types confirm directly.
  - **Approval and denial emails**, with a reason template.
- **Bulk approve/deny with reasons** as one resumable bulk operation, with progress and safe retries.
- **Group registration:** one payer, many named registrants (each with a type), plus **later substitution** of a named registrant until a cut-off. Audited, and the badge and ticket move to the new person.
- **Guest (+1)** as its own registration type linked to the host registrant. This is separate from wedding parties; check M4.1a's model for shared names only.
- The host UI is a queue with filters, plus a detail drawer with the application answers.

## Acceptance
- A bulk approve of 500 is one resumable bulk operation (kill and resume it in the test).
- A denied registrant is never charged.
- Substitution keeps exactly one valid credential.
- Capacity per type is respected on approval under concurrency.
- Isolation; impersonation and freeze coverage.
- E2E on all three projects:
  - apply, get auto-approved by domain
  - manual approve and pay
  - deny with a reason
  - group checkout with three names, then substitute one
  - +1 type
  - keyboard only, axe, RTL
  - viewer denied
