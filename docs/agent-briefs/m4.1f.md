## Scope (each fully tested)
- **Public contact collector** per event (a shareable link or QR):
  - guests submit name, household members, postal address, email and phone
  - spam-protected (rate limit plus challenge)
  - submissions land in a **host approval queue**: approve into a new party, merge into an existing party (field-by-field), or reject
  - nothing changes a party without host approval
  - address, phone and email go to sealed private fields (P4-3)
- **Invitations** sent per party by email and/or SMS through messaging, with each party's magic link.
  - Templates per event in all locales.
  - Bulk send with a preview and test send.
  - Tracked as `sent` and `viewed` (feeding M4.1d's states).
  - Bounces and failures appear on the party.
- **Deadline reminders** on the M3.7a journey engine: e.g. 14 and 3 days before the deadline. They **stop once a party answers**, and respect quiet hours.
- Guests are never added to marketing audiences (P4-3). These are transactional messages only.

## Acceptance
- Reminders stop once a party answers (a journey test with time travel).
- A collector submission never changes a party without host approval.
- A bounced email shows on the party.
- Quiet hours respected.
- Isolation; impersonation and freeze coverage. Route ownership for the collector page.
- E2E on all three projects:
  - collector submit
  - approve into a new party; merge into an existing one
  - send invitations (fake providers) and see them as sent, then viewed after opening the link
  - keyboard only, axe, RTL
  - viewer can't send
