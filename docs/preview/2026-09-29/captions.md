# Preview screenshots — 2026-09-29

Features merged since 2026-09-28, captured on a local production build (fixture org Lakeside Events, demo data only, fake payment and sign-in providers, 1280 px wide).

- `01-event-live-metrics.png` — The event home now shows live key numbers (sales, revenue, tickets sold, checked in) that update as tickets sell and guests arrive.
- `02-door-screen-live-count.png` — The check-in screen at the door updates on its own when another scanner lets someone in ("Live · 2 check-ins since you opened this page"), with no reload.
- `03-tracked-links-report.png` — Tracked links for an event, with clicks, paid orders, revenue and conversion for each link (newsletter, Instagram, partner blog).
- `04-tracked-link-attribution.png` — One tracked link's page: its QR code, results, and the orders it brought in.
- `05-messaging-limits.png` — Messaging limits: this month's email/SMS/WhatsApp/push quotas and a text message blocked because the guest never agreed to receive texts.
- `06-messaging-frequency-caps.png` — Frequency caps: the organizer sets how many messages one person can get per channel in a given time.
- `07-preference-center.png` — The guest's own preference page (reached from any email's unsubscribe link), where they choose which emails, texts and WhatsApp messages they want.
- `08-audience-template.png` — The audience builder using the "Registered, not checked in" template: the conditions it fills in and the 4 matching people.
- `09-refund-request-queue.png` — The refund-request queue: every buyer's open request across events, with how long it has waited and the answer-by date.
- `10-order-timeline.png` — One order's full timeline in the event's time zone: purchase, payment, tickets, the refund request, the decline, the second request, the refund and a team note.
- `11-cancel-wizard-money-preview.png` — The cancel/postpone wizard shows exactly how much money goes back to buyers before anything is changed, and asks for confirmation.
- `12-organizer-waitlist.png` — The organizer's waitlist for a sold-out pass: people in line order, automatic-offer settings, and "Offer now" / "Remove" buttons.
- `13-buyer-join-waitlist.png` — What a buyer sees when a pass is sold out: a simple form to join the waitlist and get an email offer if tickets free up.
- `14-seat-chart-by-date.png` — Seating for a two-night event: the second night has its own seat chart, separate from the first night's.
- `15-seat-chart-floor-plan-underlay.png` — The second night's seat chart drawn on top of an uploaded floor-plan picture of the venue (the floor plan is a demo image).
- `16-sign-in-google-apple-human-check.png` — The sign-in page with "Continue with Google" and "Continue with Apple" buttons (test provider) and the "Are you a person?" security check.
- `17-emails-all-kinds-labelled.png` — The Email templates page now names every kind of email properly, including the new waitlist and ticket-cancelled emails (the list is shown expanded for the screenshot).
- `18-arabic-audience.png` — The same audience builder in Arabic, laid out right to left.

Notes:
- Quiet hours have no settings screen: they are a fixed rule (no texts 9 PM–8 AM in the recipient's time zone), and messages held by it appear in the "Held and blocked" list on Messaging limits (05).
- The audience demo people (Ava, Ivy, Cy, Fin) and their "Harbor Gala 2028 …" events come from the project's test fixture, so the event name carries a random suffix.
